## Hi, I'm Erik

Platform and infrastructure engineer by day, security research on the side.
Most of my career is Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS, and CI/CD. I ran
Vault-as-a-service at Cisco (including the FedRAMP side) and drove Brivo's
move to org-wide Kubernetes. Currently on the data platform team at Apple.

### Research

- [fractal-basins-lab](https://github.com/terrafying/fractal-basins-lab) -
  independent reproduction of "Fractal basins trap latent reasoning" (Lai et
  al. 2026) on the EqR recurrent solver: basin maps, entropy/alpha metrics,
  plus a bit-exact early-exit solver (2.75x). Writeup in-repo.
- Leak hunting: tooling that finds exposed secrets and credentials in public
  data. Detection and disclosure only.
- Cache-key confusion: auditing caching layers for the sibling-key bug class.
- Local-lab LLM vuln work: prompt injection and agent security against
  self-hosted models, Docker loopback only.
